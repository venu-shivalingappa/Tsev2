1. PROJECT OVERVIEW 

Purpose of the Website 

The Tech Solve Engine website must position the company as a Strategic Technology Partner that designs, builds, secures, and operates the technology backbone powering business growth. 

The website must communicate that TSE: 

understands business strategy and technology 

builds scalable technology architecture 

ensures secure operations 

supports long-term business growth 

acts as a trusted technology partner 

This website should not resemble a generic IT services site. 

It must present TSE as an engineering-driven technology partner for serious organizations.
